CHINESE-SOURCE CALIBRATION RESEARCH
2026-11-04 local time; live captures dated 2026-11-05 UTC

RESULT

Found actual Chinese-commented PC source with battery-voltage calibration
commands and a separate save-calibration action. This is a concrete new lead,
not a verified fix or a manufacturer-confirmed service utility for this unit.
The associated read queries and zero-amount BTA probes produced no reply on
machine 8048 firmware 2.10. No nonzero BTA or PSAVE command was sent.

1. PUBLIC TQF-AllInOne SOURCE

Repository:
https://github.com/zhuaibai/TQF-AllInOne
Inspected revision: 82409ae4950c906634998e429dfb2372a2900603

Calibration command source:
https://github.com/zhuaibai/TQF-AllInOne/blob/82409ae4950c906634998e429dfb2372a2900603/WpfApp1/Command/Comand_GB3024/Special_Command.cs

Line 1998: SendSettingCommand("BTA+", "1") -- battery voltage increase.
Line 2086: SendSettingCommand("BTA-", "1") -- battery voltage decrease.
Line 1294: SendSettingCommand("PSAVE", "") -- separate save-calibration action.
The battery regions are named 电池电压+ and 电池电压-.
AC-voltage controls also call IVA+ / IVA- with "01". Their method names and
comments are inconsistent enough that their direction should not be inferred
without verification. No IVA command was sent.

The settings path pauses background polling and waits two seconds before
sending. This motivated the five-second quiet-interval tests of the known
CSCA battery command. It does not prove such a pause is required by firmware.

Serial framing source:
https://github.com/zhuaibai/TQF-AllInOne/blob/82409ae4950c906634998e429dfb2372a2900603/WpfApp1/Services/SerialCommunicationService.cs

SendCommand sends the supplied ASCII query bytes; the callers include CR.
SendSettingCommand concatenates command and value, computes CRC16/CCITT with
initial value zero, emits the high CRC byte first, increments CRC bytes equal
to 0A/0D/28, and appends CR. Thus BTA+1 is CRC-framed in this application.

Protocol selection source:
https://github.com/zhuaibai/TQF-AllInOne/blob/82409ae4950c906634998e429dfb2372a2900603/WpfApp1/ViewModels/MainWindowVM.cs

Queries QPRTL<CR>; recognizes HPVINV/LPVINV-family identifiers and other device
types. We did not find VPHB, CSCA or CSICAL in this repository. Those are the
working status family and attempted calibration family of the user's unit.
The repository is public author code, not established as an official release
or evidence of identical boards/firmware. No downloaded installer was run.

2. PRIMARY REFERENCE FOR A ZERO-AMOUNT BTA PROBE

MPP Solar PCM3012 MPPT calibration SOP, DIFFERENT HARDWARE:
https://www.mppsolar.com/v3/catalogs/pcm3012type05/MPPT%20Calibration%20SOP.pdf

Documents BTA+XX/BTA-XX with integer amounts 0 through 20, and shows CRC use
and an ACK in its example. This supplied the zero-amount BTA+00 probe syntax.
Its 12 V test setup and calibration scaling must not be assumed applicable
to the user's nominal 48 V inverter.

An independent implementation also documents BTA in its PI30 command set:
https://github.com/jblance/mpp-solar/blob/eafdd4328c77f516cf82430c5bcb6fa042c45d3a/mppsolar/protocols/pi30.py

This confirms use on that protocol family, not compatibility with VPHB/2.10.
Other inverter protocols use different BTA formats, including absolute/two-
point calibration. No such command, calibration reset, or save was attempted.

3. LIVE RESULTS DERIVED FROM THESE LEADS

parser-tqf-read-20261105T014934Z.*
QPRTL, HIMSG1, HBAT, HEEP1, HEEP2, HOP and HGRID, each tested plain CR and
CRC-plus-CR. Fourteen probes: no reply to any, while normal VPHB health checks
continued to succeed and configuration remained unchanged.

parser-bta-zero-20261105T015118Z.*
Two calibration WRITE probes with requested amount zero:
  BTA+00 plus CRC and CR: 42 54 41 2B 30 30 20 BD 0D
  BTA+00 plus CR:         42 54 41 2B 30 30 0D
Neither elicited a reply or observable voltage/configuration change.
This does not test the exact one-digit, nonzero BTA+1 call in TQF-AllInOne,
and silence alone cannot prove that every BTA format is unsupported.
There is no confirmed accepted BTA write to reverse or commit; PSAVE was
not sent. No firmware lock/unlock conclusion follows from these results.

calibration-quiet-effect-20261105T015320Z.*
calibration-quiet-effect-20261105T015849Z.*
Original vendor CSCA/CSICAL command with five-second quiet intervals before
and after raw 130 (+0.5% in PC software), followed by raw 125. No change in
either battery voltage (52.1 V) or calibration field (125). The latter test
reported 0% load throughout. No extra ACK/NAK/error bytes were received.

The output-off experiment obtained no baseline status reply and therefore
sent no calibration write. Output and communication were restored afterward.
See calibration-investigation.txt for the complete experiment record.

NEXT MISSING EVIDENCE

A successful capture, service procedure, matching firmware image for offline
inspection, or alternate utility that explicitly supports machine 8048,
inverter firmware 2.10 and its VPHB/CSCA interface. Shared branding or board
appearance alone does not establish command equivalence. Until an actual
adjustment is observed, persistence and the other five calibration controls
remain unverified. No vendor message was sent and no firmware was flashed.
